# Asset provenance

## Generated product images
`assets/paste.webp`, `oil.webp`, `brush.webp` were generated with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on October 25, 2026 for this fictional demo. They do not depict actual goods offered for sale. The original exact generation prompts are embedded in EXIF ImageDescription and retained in local JSON sidecars. Images optimized to 900px WebP; no creative edits were made during conversion. Packaging lettering is generated artwork; the demo does not claim a registered trademark.

BEFORE YOU ROLL IN.

Walk-in or appointment?

Both are welcome in this example. Joining the line is an estimated place, not a guaranteed time. Appointments are reserved separately. This demo creates neither.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. The sample kids cut is $24 for ages 5–12 and takes 30 minutes. A parent or guardian stays with the child.

Designs, patterns and hot towels?

All on the board. A simple design is $18 / 20 minutes as an add-on. Bring a reference; more involved ideas need a separate quote in a real shop. Hot towel shave is $30 / 30 minutes.

Cash or card?

A real version could accept both. This example takes no payment and never asks for card details.

Can you remind me in a few weeks?

The demo booking flow can show a reminder preview for two or three weeks after your cut. It sends no message, saves no number and grants no marketing consent.

What if I miss my place?

Example policy: check in within 10 minutes of your demo alert, or the chair moves to the next person. Leave the demo line any time. Real wait times can change.

What if my usual barber is away?

Pick first available. A real shared client record could carry your preferences between chairs. Here, the fictional barbers share one service menu; no client record is created.

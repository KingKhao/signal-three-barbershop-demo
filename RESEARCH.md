# Barbershop reference research — October 25, 2026

Started with https://colorlib.com/wp/barbershop-websites/ (dated October 15, 2026) and https://www.booknetic.com/blog/barbershop-website-template-examples (May 10, 2026). These are editorial/promotional collections, not an independently established ranking or evidence of conversion performance.

Directly opened and rendered:
- https://www.fortworthbarbershop.com/ — shop interior dominates first viewport, distinctive stacked blue identity, clear booking; narrative names the people behind the chairs. Adapt physical-shop specificity and large lettering, not its assets, palette or full-screen photo structure.
- https://www.crowsnestbarbershop.com/ — large cream/yellow wordmark on dark ground, assertive booking action. Mobile uses tall photographic coverage and compact navigation. Adapt immediate brand confidence and clear booking priority; replace location-driven navigation with the demo chair board.
- https://scissorsscotch.com/home — photographic interior hero, booking/services/memberships directly in navigation. Memberships are a distinct offer in the page content. The reference's own entrance motion delays its headline; our information remains visible by default.

No forms submitted. No logos, customer quotes, images or code copied. Reference screenshots retained outside the deliverable. The page uses requested Unsplash URLs as illustrative stock; stock models are never presented as authentic barbers.

Chosen direction: a queue-first, three-chair dispatch board. Signal Three's three chairs become the recurring structure: chair board, chair selection, ticket, barber row and three retail items. Price board is the center of the scroll, not another card section. Large condensed lettering, charcoal/cream, chartreuse, hard rectangular photos. No arches, serif headlines, postcard layers or slow parallax from Sunday House.
